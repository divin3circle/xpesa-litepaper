01 — Overview

What is xpesa?

xpesa is a creator monetization platform built for African creators. It puts a paywall in front of any content a creator already has — a Google Drive folder, a Notion page, a Telegram group invite, a Zoom link. And lets fans pay with USDC from any chain via gas sponsored smart accounts. Creators receive payment in USDC and can withdraw directly to fiat in one click.

xpesa is also a tipping layer: creators can drop a tip link in their bio, newsletter, or post and receive open-ended appreciation payments from their community. Both modes run on the same infrastructure.

The pitch to African creators is not about crypto. It is: "You get paid in dollars. You withdraw to fiat same day. You never chase anyone on WhatsApp for money again."

🔗

Drop any piece of content behind a price. Fan pays USDC, gets the content. Creator never exposes the uploaded content until payment clears.

☕

A shareable pay-what-you-want page for every creator. Goes in a bio, email footer, or tweet. No content required.

📱

📊

Transaction history, conversion stats, link performance, and AI-powered insights in one clean view.

02 — Problem

The African creator pain

Content creators, educators, coaches, and freelancers across Kenya produce real value every day. Monetizing that value is painful, manual, and has a lot of friction. The current process looks like this:

Step 1Post content or offer
→
Step 2"DM me to pay via Mobile money/Bank"
→
Step 3Wait. Chase. Confirm manually
→
Step 4DM the resource individually
→
Step 5Track it in a notebook

xpesa collapses steps 2–5 to zero effort. That's the entire product thesis.

Why existing platforms don't work

Gumroad / Payhip

No instant mobile money withdrawal. USD-only payouts. Slow international transfers. Designed for Western creators with Western bank accounts.

Ko-fi / Buy Me a Coffee

No crypto, no M-Pesa. Kenyan creators use them but can't easily cash out. Platform can freeze accounts with no recourse.

Manual M-Pesa

Free but completely unscalable. No automation, no delivery, no analytics. Works for 10 sales, breaks at 100.

Raw Crypto Wallets

QR codes, gas errors, wrong networks. Abandonment rate near 100% for non-crypto-native audiences.

Who feels this pain most acutely

The early xpesa market is not influencers with brand deals — it is the side-hustle creator: campus students selling notes, Twitter/X educators selling guides, coaches selling templates, developers selling boilerplates. These people are already monetizing manually. xpesa is a better workflow, not a new behaviour.

03 — Solution

How xpesa works

The core mechanic

A creator authenticates with email/wallet, creates a content link (gated or tip), sets a price, and shares the xpesa URL. When a fan visits, they connect theri smart wallets via social accounts (ThirdWeb SDK — supports email-Avalanched embedded wallets so crypto knowledge is not required), sign a payment request, and the USDC transfer settles on Avalanche in ~15 seconds. Once payment is confirmed the user signs the last access message to assert that their wallet is the one that made the payment and the content is revealed.

Tip mode — no content required

A creator can create a tip link with no destination URL — just a title, a suggested amount, and their wallet address. The fan pays, sees a thank-you screen and a special message, and the creator receives USDC.

04 — Monetization Models

Content Gate & Tip

xpesa launches with two monetization modes that share the same payment infrastructure. Every creator link is one or the other.

🔒
Content Gate

Fixed price. Fan pays exactly the set amount and gets access to the underlying content(URL, Document, Audio, Video etc). Access is logged per wallet. Expiry is set by creator: 1-time, 1 hour, 1 month, forever.

💚
Tip / PWYW

Pay what you want. Creator sets a suggested amount or minimum of zero. No content gate — fan pays, gets a thank-you screen. Creator builds goodwill, gets USDC.

How they look on a creator's profile

A creator's xpesa page is a simple list of their active links — both gated and tip items coexist. Fans see the title, price/tip suggestion, and a pay button. One profile, one wallet address, two modes.

06 — AI Feature Roadmap

Intelligence, phased by usefulness

AI features in xpesa are not decorative. Each one is mapped to a specific creator friction point and introduced at the moment it becomes relevant — not before.

0 1
MVP

AI Link Description Writer

Creator uploads content or writes a short description of what they're selling. AI generates a compelling title and 2-sentence description for the xpesa payment page. Removes the blank-page problem for first-time creators who are not copywriters. A single prompt, no conversation history needed.

0 2
Month 2

AI Smart Pricing Suggestions

When a creator sets a price, AI analyzes: content category (notes vs template vs consultation vs community access), creator's audience size if they connect their Twitter/X, and comparable items already on xpesa. Returns a suggested price range with reasoning. Reduces the anxiety of "am I charging too much?" which is one of the top reasons creators undercharge or don't charge at all.

0 3
Month 3

AI Conversational Dashboard Insights

Instead of a static analytics page, the creator dashboard includes an AI assistant that surfaces actionable observations: "Your revision notes link got 40 visits but only 8 payments this week — 20% conversion. Similar links convert at 35%. Your price may be slightly high for your audience size. Want to try a lower price for 7 days?"

0 4
Month 5

AI Fraud & Anomaly Detection

Lightweight ML model flags unusual transaction patterns: same wallet purchasing the same link 10+ times, a single link receiving 500 visits from one IP range, a new creator account suddenly processing 100× typical volume. These flags trigger manual review, not auto-suspension. Critical for platform trust as volume grows. Can start as rules-Avalanched and migrate to a model later.

0 5
Month 6

AI Content Category Auto-Tagging

As xpesa grows toward a discoverable marketplace, AI automatically categorizes creator links by type (education, templates, community, consultation, code, etc.) Avalanched on title and description. Enables browse/search for buyers without creators needing to manually tag. Required before any marketplace or discovery layer is viable.

0 6
Year 2

AI Agentic Tipping (MCP Server)

xpesa exposes an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server so AI agents — Claude, AutoGPT, custom bots — can tip or purchase content on behalf of users. This is the Smart accounts protocol's natural endpoint: any agent that can make HTTP requests can pay for content. Positions xpesa as tipping infrastructure for the agentic web, not just a creator tool.

12 — Risks & Mitigations

Risk register

RiskSeverityMitigation
Buyer wallet friction — non-crypto fans abandon payment because wallet setup is too complex High ThirdWeb smart wallets reduce this to zero crypto knowledge required.
Link sharing weakness — fan pays, gets Google Drive, shares it with friends Medium Deliberate product tradeoff. Mitigate by encouraging creators to use password-protected or access-controlled destinations (Notion, private Google Drive). For high-value content, recommend access-expiry settings.
Kotani Pay dependency — single offramp provider for M-Pesa; outages or API changes break withdrawals High Integrate Mara and Transak as fallback offramp providers from Phase 2 onwards. Abstract offramp into a provider interface so switching is a config change, not a rewrite.
CBK regulatory action — Central Bank of Kenya moves against USDC payments or crypto rails Medium xpesa is non-custodial — creator wallets are self-managed, funds never touch xpesa's accounts. Kotani Pay holds the relevant money-transmission license for the KES conversion. xpesa is a "creator tools software" not "crypto payment app".
Manual M-Pesa inertia — creators with existing audiences don't switch because their fans already know the DM workflow Medium Target creators who are just starting to monetize — they have no existing workflow to switch from. For established creators, pitch xpesa as an additional channel, not a replacement. The tip link in a bio is additive, not disruptive.
USDC gas costs on Avalanche — gas spike makes small transactions uneconomical Low Avalanche gas is ~$0.001 per transfer. Even at 100× spike this is $0.10 — still viable for a $1+ transaction. Display gas estimate before confirmation so fans aren't surprised.
Content policy abuse — creators gate illegal or harmful content behind xpesa links Medium Creator ToS with clear prohibited content list. Report mechanism on every payment page. Ability to disable a link within minutes. Since xpesa never serves the content directly liability is similar to a URL shortener, not a content host.
